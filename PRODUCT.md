# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Seth and interview reviewers evaluating OpenAI SDR / Strategic BDR interview materials. The primary context is high-stakes interview preparation, Loom recording, and quick review of concise supporting artifacts.

## Product Purpose

This repo is an interview-prep knowledge base and artifact workspace. Success means source-backed answers, polished presentation materials, and rehearsal-ready outputs that help Seth communicate enterprise sales judgment clearly.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, executive.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, over-decorated dashboard cards, loud accent palettes, unsupported claims, and dense script pasted directly onto slides.

## Design Principles

- Use source-backed content and visible truth boundaries.
- Prefer executive clarity over decorative flourish.
- Let spacing, hierarchy, and restraint carry the design.
- Keep artifacts speaker-led and easy to review under time pressure.
- Preserve existing canonical prep documents unless explicitly asked to edit them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-operable artifacts, reduced-motion support, and text sizes appropriate for screen-shared interview review.
