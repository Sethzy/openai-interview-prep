## Canonical Answer

The DoorDash case should be:

> DoorDash should use OpenAI to move AI from consumer discovery into local operations, starting with a multilingual voice workflow that recovers stalled Dashers, reduces repeat support loops, improves escalation quality, and turns messy activation friction into structured operating data.

The proof artifact is:

> A simulated Dasher reactivation voice demo. It is not real DoorDash infrastructure. It shows how the workflow could feel, where guardrails sit, and how an operator could critique a PoC.

## Question-First Recording Script

Use this as the main rehearsal script. The `Question` line is a checkpoint, not necessarily something to say out loud. Each answer includes the slide to show so the recording does not drift.

### Part 1: Why You / Why OpenAI

#### Question: Why OpenAI?

*Show slide 1, then show the following artifacts.*

Script answer:

> Hi, my name is Seth.
> 
> I'm applying to OAI believe everyone should have Codex working on their behalf. 
> 
> I use it every day—as a second brain, to build software, to scrape leads
> 
> And most recently, I even used it to make birthday cards.
> 
> I want to join OpenAI to help enterprises find similarly valuable use cases that solves problems within their businesses. 

#### Question: Why this role?

I’m drawn to this role because it combines enterprise account work and technical discovery—the intersection where I’ve done my best work.

#### Question: What makes you a strong fit?

*Show slide 2: what makes me a strong fit.*

At Boxo, I was the founding enterprise sales lead across the Philippines, Vietnam and Malaysia. At Airwallex, I was the second outbound hire in Singapore and hit 200% of quota.

On the technical side, I deployed enterprise healthcare workflows with a YC startup in San Francisco and, as a founder, built agentic systems end to end. 

That combination helps me translate OpenAI's technical capability into customer confidence.

#### Question: Why would you thrive here?

> I would thrive because I have a deep curiosity over AI. I am consistently keeping up with the frontier.
> 
> CLIs vs MCPs. Software factories. Etc. 
> 
> The pace of progress is unrelenting but that means there's always something interesting to learn.

### Part 2: Advise DoorDash

#### Question: How should DoorDash use AI over the next 3 years?

*Slide to show: Slide 4, `DoorDash Global Coverage`.* 

Script answer:

My three-year view is that DoorDash should use AI to make its global operations cheaper and more reliable.

DoorDash operates across more than 40 countries, but delivery businesses have thin unit economics.

*Slide to show: Slide 5, `DoorDash Profitability Challenges`.*

In Q1 2026, for every $100 customers spent through DoorDash, it recorded about $12.80 as revenue and roughly 60 cents as net income.

At these margins, every cent saved on operations matters.

#### Question: What is the one high-impact OpenAI use case?

*Slide to show: Slide 6, `Problem Statement`.*

Script answer:

This is because running a three-sided marketplace is expensive: consumers, merchants and Dashers all need consistent support across different languages, policies and local exceptions.

*Slide to show: Slide 7, `Multilingual Voice Agent for Dasher Reactivation`.*

The high-impact use case I’d recommend starting with is a multilingual local-operations voice agent, starting with Dasher reactivation.

The agent would follow approved country SOPs, speak the Dasher’s preferred language and hand urgent cases to a human operator.

I would build a business case on the four metrics show on the slide. The key question is whether voice agents can improve the economics of reactivation.

#### Question: How would the workflow work, and where are the guardrails?

*Slide to show: Slide 8, `Voice Agent Contact Center Workflow`.*

Script answer:
> 
> > I also built a short demo to show how it would work. 
> 

| Cue        | Exact action or line                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Start      | Click `Connect`.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Maya       | “Hi, this is Maya calling from DoorDash. Is this Seth?”                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Seth 1** | “Yes, this is Seth.”                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Maya       | “Thanks, Seth. You completed your first dash two weeks ago but haven't dashed since. What got in the way?”                                                                                                 |
| **Seth 2** | “alamak, 我以为 first dash then can cash out already leh. 为什么  fasty pay 还是 locked?”                                                                                                                          |
| Logs       | Wait for `lookup_payout`, then `check_fast_pay_eligibility`.                                                                                                                                               |
| Interrupt  | Let Maya finish the payout sentence. As she starts talking about Fast Pay, speak over her.                                                                                                                 |
| **Seth 3** | “Wait, what’s the difference between weekly payout and Fast Pay?”                                                                                                                                          |
| Maya       | “Weekly payout is the automatic bank deposit, and yours already went through. Fast Pay lets you cash out instantly; you need 23 more deliveries.”                                                          |
| **Seth 4** | “Okay, cool. That makes sense.”                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Maya + log | Maya checks `check_return_challenge`, calls it a **return offer**, then says: “Complete five eligible deliveries by Sunday, July 19 at 11:59 PM, and you can earn an extra $25. Any questions about that?” |
| **Seth 5** | “No, that’s all.”                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Close      | Wait for `write_crm_outcome` and: “Glad we cleared that up. Thanks for your time, Seth.”                                                                                                                   |
| End        | Hold the logs for one second, then click `Disconnect`.                                                                                                                                                     |

As the demo shows, I believe GPT-Realtime-2.1 can credibly handle a merchant reactivation. 

**Show Demo Interface — target 35 to 45 seconds.**

#### Question: If you had 30 days to build momentum, what would you do?

*Slide to show: Slide 9, `Potential Champions`.*

> If I had 30 days, I would turn this hypothesis and demo into a concrete, champion-led PoC path.

I would start outreach with operators because they know where activation actually breaks. 

*Slide to show: Slide 10, `Sample Outreach`.*

For outreach, I would tailor it to the business priorities of each stakeholder.

For example:

For Dasher Operations, I would lead with reducing manual touches per successful reactivation. For technical stakeholders, I would lead with handoff accuracy; for the economic sponsor, cost per reactivation.

In discovery, I would clarify where activation fails, what it costs, and solutions they've already tried.

*Slide to show: Slide 11, `30-Day Path to a Sponsor-Backed PoC`.*

Once I've validated the problem, I would sharpen the workflow with the champion and unblock technical and security considerations. When ready, I would arrange a meeting with our champion and the economic sponsor to jointly pitch the business case. 

*Slide to show: Slide 12, `What We Need From the Champion`.* 

To help the champion progress this internally, I would work with them on a checklist of things: bringing the right people into the process, providing approved SOPs, and align on success metrics. 

#### Question: What would you do that others likely would not?

My standout approach is that I can build technical workflow diagrams and demos that shows we understand the customers issues deeply. 
> 
In my experience, tangible proof is what moves the conversation from interest to a decision.
