## Reusable Takeaways

1. Prepare a concrete answer for the first 30, 60, and 90 days.
2. Expect the final interviewer to test practical sales execution: prospecting, sequence design, pipeline generation, account selection, deal inspection, and multi-threading.
3. The ramp answer should name outputs at each stage rather than remaining a generic learning plan.
4. Any numeric ramp targets should be calibrated to the manager's actual expectations instead of copied from an outsider's example.
5. Market selection should be evidence-led and account-specific. The transcript's country generalizations are not safe interview claims.

## Applied Prep Decision

The active role-play pack now treats the ramp as:

- **Days 1–30:** calibrate, learn the territory and motion, align with internal partners, and produce a priority-account thesis plus first campaign.
- **Days 31–60:** execute with feedback, create live account conversations, lead appropriate discovery, and produce the first opportunities that the Account Directors consider worth advancing.
- **Days 61–90:** improve conversion, build repeatability, contribute qualified pipeline, and document one useful AI-enabled GTM workflow.

The answer deliberately avoids presenting ten meetings, five opportunities, or a fixed percentage of Tier 1 penetration as OpenAI expectations because the transcript itself frames those as examples.
