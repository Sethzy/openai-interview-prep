# Source: OpenAI State Of Enterprise AI 2025 Report

Navigation: [[index]] | [[sources/_index|Sources]] | [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]]

## Source Boundary

This source note captures the report's interview-useful thesis rather than
every statistic. Use it to support enterprise adoption maturity, not to recite
numbers from memory.

## Key Claims

- OpenAI and Wharton frame the report around enterprise AI moving from
  experimentation toward business impact.
- The report is based on a large global survey of more than 9,000 leaders and
  employees, plus expert and executive interviews.
- The report says AI usage increased significantly from 2024 to 2025.
- The report says many employees report efficiency gains, and many
  organizations report ROI and workforce improvements.
- The report's more useful interview claim is qualitative: AI value comes from
  integrating AI into workflows, operating cadence, measurement, governance,
  and employee behavior.
- This strengthens the internal-build answer because it shows that enterprise
  value is not merely "we have an AI team." The hard part is adoption depth.

## Interview Use

Use this as the adoption-maturity layer:

> The enterprise AI report is useful because it makes the problem bigger than
> model access. A company can have internal AI teams and still be early in
> adoption if usage is fragmented, pilots are not measured, or workflows are
> not changing. OpenAI can help customers move from experimentation to actual
> operating change.

## Guardrails

- Do not cite exact report percentages unless rechecked that day.
- Do not treat the report as independent proof that every OpenAI deployment
  succeeds.
- Use it to support workflow integration, measurement, and adoption maturity.
