## Assignment Shape

The assignment is a 7-minute-or-less Loom. It has two sections:

1. Part 1, 1-2 minutes: introduce yourself to the hiring panel.
2. Part 2, 5 minutes: advise a strategic customer and create executive interest in partnering with OpenAI.

The prompt explicitly allows ChatGPT or other AI tools. The evaluation focus is not whether AI was used, but how effectively the candidate uses tools to think and communicate.

## What OpenAI Says It Is Testing

The email says the assignment evaluates:

- thinking;
- communication;
- trust-building;
- operating in a high-impact environment.

It also names these qualities:

- genuine passion for AI and OpenAI's mission;
- executive presence;
- communication skills;
- strategic thinking;
- business judgment;
- consultative instincts;
- customer empathy;
- creativity;
- resourcefulness;
- ownership mentality;
- long-term ambition.

## Part 1 Requirements

The opening should briefly cover:

- why OpenAI;
- why this role;
- what makes the candidate a strong fit;
- why the candidate would thrive in the environment.

The named tone signals are:

- conviction;
- self-awareness;
- humility;
- ambition;
- authenticity.

## Part 2 Requirements

The strategic-customer prompt names DoorDash for Stanley's process.

Required coverage:

1. Point of view: how DoorDash should use AI to strengthen its business or unlock future growth over the next 3 years.
2. One high-impact use case: one practical OpenAI use case, with examples including customer experience, merchant growth, Dasher operations, internal productivity, support, and logistics.
3. Executive engagement plan for the first 30 days:
   - who to target first and why;
   - what business priorities to lead with;
   - how to earn credibility;
   - what outreach would sound like;
   - at least one example of outbound messaging.
4. Standout approach:
   - what the candidate would do that others likely would not;
   - which strengths would give the candidate an edge.

Do not assume the same dates, recruiter, assignment link, account, or review cadence for Seth.

## Key Prep Implication

This prompt confirms the Loom is not just a product pitch. It is a mini executive-account-strategy exercise.

Seth's submission should therefore combine:

- a tight personal opening;
- a clear account point of view;
- one high-impact OpenAI use case;
- a first-30-days engagement plan;
- a concrete outbound message;
- a standout approach grounded in Seth's actual strengths.
