## Quick Verdict

Useful, but mostly as strategy texture.

The strongest reusable points are:

- APAC enterprise selling should start from workflows and departments, not generic company logos.
- In Southeast Asia, pure engineering-seat selling may be harder outside a few large or unusually technical accounts because developer-cost arbitrage changes the ROI story.
- Knowledge work, finance operations, customer operations, support, voice, and internal company-context workflows look like stronger regional wedges.
- Customer-support and voice-agent activity from vendors such as Sierra, HappyRobot, and Deepgram is useful market-signal texture for the voice AI CSR thesis, but it still needs external verification before live use.
- The "connect internal systems, reason over company context, and turn SOPs into shared workflows" wedge is useful for explaining ChatGPT Enterprise, Workspace Agents, connectors, and Codex as a work surface.
