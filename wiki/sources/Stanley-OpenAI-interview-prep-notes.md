## Key Extraction

The source adds a candidate-ready answer bank that complements the process briefing. It does not add new process stages; it adds stronger ways to answer the most repeated questions.

Most useful answer patterns:

- Use one consistent throughline in the elevator pitch, e.g. "I keep joining early/no-playbook GTM teams and building repeatable motion."
- Anchor "why OpenAI" in a personal AI usage story plus the APAC build-out.
- Explain the AE-to-SDR step-back as a deliberate long-game decision, not a fallback.
- Handle compensation and promotion pushback with five-year trajectory and merit language.
- Use a four-part prospecting motion: research, prioritise, sequence, iterate.
- Target by buying motion, not generic personas: org-wide productivity, builder/API, governance/deployment.
- Discuss competitors respectfully and avoid "our model is better" as the main argument.
- Explain AI commercially through adoption waves, not deep model internals.
