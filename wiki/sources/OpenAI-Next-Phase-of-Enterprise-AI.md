# Source: OpenAI: The Next Phase Of Enterprise AI

Navigation: [[index]] | [[sources/_index|Sources]] | [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]]

## Source Boundary

This is the clearest official source for explaining the enterprise AI stack in
business language. It supports a more concrete internal-build answer by naming
three layers: Business Context, Agent Execution, and Evaluation and
Optimization.

## Key Claims

- OpenAI frames enterprise AI as moving from simple assistance toward systems
  that understand business context, perform work, and improve through
  measurement.
- Business Context means bringing relevant company context from connected
  systems into ChatGPT while respecting permissions.
- Agent Execution means allowing AI to perform pre-authorized workflows through
  controlled connectors and MCP-style integrations.
- Evaluation and Optimization means measuring and improving behavior before
  scaling.
- The strongest interview inference is that OpenAI is building the enterprise
  layers around the model: context, action, identity/permissions, evaluation,
  and governance.
- This is the cleanest vocabulary for the internal-build objection because it
  answers "what are we buying?" without sounding abstract.

## Interview Use

Use this as the backbone:

> The way I would frame it is: internal teams should own the business logic,
> policy, workflow, and data model. OpenAI helps with the platform layers that
> are hard to keep rebuilding: business context, agent execution, evaluation
> and optimization, identity and permissions, and governance. That is the
> difference between a demo and something a large enterprise can actually run.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply all Agent Execution features are generally available for every
  customer.
- Do not overclaim app connectors, MCP access, or research-preview scope.
- Use the layer names as a mental model, then validate current packaging with
  the OpenAI team.
