# Source: OpenAI Frontier

Navigation: [[index]] | [[sources/_index|Sources]] | [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]]

## Source Boundary

This is a compact interview-prep source note for OpenAI's Frontier enterprise
page. It supports the "why partner with OpenAI if we have internal AI teams?"
answer by showing OpenAI's enterprise motion as platform plus expertise, not
only API access.

## Key Claims

- OpenAI presents Frontier as an advanced AI platform for enterprises.
- The page frames Frontier around OpenAI models and products, one-to-one
  technical expertise, and flexible deployment options.
- The page points to two enterprise paths:
  - platform access to OpenAI models and products such as ChatGPT, Codex, Sora,
    APIs, and tools;
  - custom deployments where OpenAI experts help design, build, and integrate
    AI agents and applications.
- The page references customer success, solutions engineering, deployment
  engineering, and research expertise as part of the support model.
- The strongest interview inference is that OpenAI can help customers avoid
  rebuilding every part of the agent and deployment stack themselves.

## Interview Use

Use this to make the internal-build answer concrete:

> I would not tell an internal AI team to stop building. I would ask what they
> want to own. Frontier is a good example of OpenAI's enterprise direction:
> platform access plus technical expertise and custom deployment support. The
> customer can own the proprietary workflow and business logic, while OpenAI
> helps with the platform layer and deployment patterns.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply every customer gets Frontier or custom deployments.
- Do not promise FDEs, research previews, or specific support resources.
- Say "OpenAI's enterprise direction" or "where applicable" when referencing
  Frontier in an interview.
