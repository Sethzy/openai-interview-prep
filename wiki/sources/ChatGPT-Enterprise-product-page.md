# Source: ChatGPT Enterprise Product Page

Navigation: [[index]] | [[sources/_index|Sources]] | [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]]

## Source Boundary

This source note captures ChatGPT Enterprise as the governed employee work
surface in OpenAI's enterprise story. It supports the internal-build answer by
separating broad employee adoption from internal AI platform work.

## Key Claims

- ChatGPT Enterprise is positioned as a business work surface with advanced
  models, tools, security, privacy, and admin controls.
- The page emphasizes that business data is not used for training.
- The page highlights company knowledge and connectors as ways to bring
  enterprise context into ChatGPT.
- The page references enterprise administration and governance concepts such as
  SSO/SAML, SCIM, RBAC, domain verification, analytics, compliance APIs, data
  retention controls, encryption, and data residency controls where available.
- The page includes business-user tooling beyond chat, such as data analysis,
  deep research, projects/canvas-style collaboration, GPTs, Codex, Sora, and
  other work surfaces, subject to packaging and availability.
- This helps distinguish the employee-adoption layer from the developer/API
  layer. A company may build internally and still need a governed employee AI
  surface.

## Interview Use

Use this to make the answer more concrete:

> Even if the buyer has an internal AI team, there is still the question of
> broad employee adoption. ChatGPT Enterprise gives the organization a governed
> work surface with admin controls, connectors, company knowledge, and privacy
> commitments. Internal teams can still own proprietary workflows while OpenAI
> helps standardize how employees use AI safely.
