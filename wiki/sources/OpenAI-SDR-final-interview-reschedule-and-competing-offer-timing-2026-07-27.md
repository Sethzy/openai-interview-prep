# OpenAI SDR Final Interview Reschedule And Competing-Offer Timing

## User-Confirmed Update

On 2026-07-27, Seth reported that:

- OpenAI moved his final interview back by two days.
- A competing company has been waiting while the OpenAI process continues.
- The competing company expects Seth to sign its offer by early the following week.

The exact rescheduled interview date and exact competing-offer deadline were not supplied in this update and should not be invented. Any recruiter email should replace placeholders with the specific calendar date before sending.

## Earlier Recruiter Context

The July 15 recruiter email supplied by Seth shows that OpenAI recruiting already knew he had another competing offer and thanked him for his patience. This means a concise deadline update is expected process information, not a new pressure tactic.

## Recommended Communication Strategy

- Reply on the most recent scheduling thread so the recruiter and recruiting coordinator share the same context.
- State the competing-offer signing deadline directly and precisely.
- Acknowledge the two-day scheduling change without sounding aggrieved.
- If true, say OpenAI is the opportunity Seth is most excited about; otherwise use the narrower truth that he remains very interested.
- Ask what decision timing may look like after the final conversation so Seth can manage the other process responsibly.
- Mention that references are ready and offer to share them if useful. Do not send unsolicited personal contact information or imply that reference checks are already part of the confirmed process.
- Confirm each reference has agreed before sharing their details.

## Truth Boundary

- The final-round invitation does not confirm a reference-check stage or decision timeline.
- References are a readiness signal, not evidence that Seth has reached an offer stage.
- Do not describe the competing offer as expiring, withdrawn, or non-negotiable unless the other company has said so.
- Do not call OpenAI the first choice unless Seth means it and would act consistently with that statement.
