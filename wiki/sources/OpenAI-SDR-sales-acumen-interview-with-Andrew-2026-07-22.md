## Positive Evidence

- The introduction led with Boxo enterprise prospecting and closing, Airwallex quota-carrying performance, then AI-native work and motivation. Andrew never raised the July 17 builder-versus-seller objection.
- The Fortune 500 answer showed mature account thinking: workflow hypotheses, external evidence, problem prioritization, stakeholder mapping, Account Director coordination, tailored outreach, adjacent proof, and a lightweight prototype.
- Andrew explicitly said the approach was “great,” praised using OpenAI tools at multiple stages, and highlighted the POC/MVP as an increasingly effective internal prospecting differentiator.
- The technical-growth answer showed deliberate daily practice through product experimentation and small builds.
- The favorite-feature answer was personal and memorable: helping Seth's mother research air conditioners and using voice to externalize and improve interview thinking.
- The UNA Financial answer was the strongest answer in the interview. It covered the sourcing signal, champion motivation, build-versus-buy resistance, failure to multi-thread early, direct technical and executive alignment, and commercial result.
- Andrew called the deal answer “really good.”
- Seth asked a strong strategic question spanning developer sales, knowledge-worker adoption, and custom deployment, which produced a detailed answer.

## Friction

- The 30-day answer was a strong account strategy but not a literal day-by-day or week-by-week plan with defined outputs.
- The Copilot objection began with features, benchmark claims, pricing speculation, and product superiority. Andrew explicitly called that route dangerous because it creates a bake-off and makes the seller transactional.
- Seth eventually reached the better discovery frame—understand the use case and gaps—but only after the feature comparison.
- Describing himself as “non-technical” undersold the demonstrated fluency. The real development area is technical depth and precision.
- UNA was stated as USD 250K fixed and roughly USD 350K by departure, conflicting with the verified boundary of approximately USD 200K ARR expanding to approximately USD 300K.
- The phrasing around “casually dropping by” the Philippines can sound manipulative even though the substantive move—creating direct executive and technical alignment—was strong.

- “That was really good” on the UNA Financial deal answer.
