# Source: InvestInAssets Sales One-Pager

Navigation: [[index]] | [[sources/_index|Sources]] |
[[OpenAI-SDR-external-source-summary-and-Lauren-final-relevance-2026-07-26]]

- **Summary:** The seven-point note argues for action over overcomplication,
  people-and-problem-first selling, urgent pain over optional benefit, trust
  built through authentic usefulness, and complete separation between the
  seller's internal targets and the buyer's problem.
- **Lauren-final relevance / takeaway:** **High** — The durable interview
  version is: start with the customer's problem, translate value into time,
  money, risk, or confidence, be useful enough to earn trust, and never make
  the buyer responsible for your quota. This supports Seth's seller-first
  positioning and discovery judgment. Treat the “four things,”
  aspirin/vitamin, and friendship lines as memorable heuristics rather than
  universal laws; enterprise proof, competence, and buying-process discipline
  still matter.
