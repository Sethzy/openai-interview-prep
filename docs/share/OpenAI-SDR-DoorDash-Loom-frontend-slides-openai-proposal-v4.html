High-impact use case03 / 09
Recommended practical OpenAI use case

Multilingual Dasher activation voice layer.

Start with one operating workflow: a Dasher completed onboarding or an early delivery, then went inactive because a blocker was not resolved clearly.

Problem surface

Supply quality
  • Acquisition effort is already spent. The business still needs reliable supply.
  • Blockers can be repetitive but sensitive. Payout, Fast Pay, background-check, and first-delivery issues require policy boundaries.
  • Language and local context matter. The same workflow should be locally intelligible without becoming uncontrolled.

Use-case mechanics

Bounded voice workflow
1

Detect

Identify a stalled Dasher after onboarding or first delivery.

2

Call

Voice agent discovers the blocker in the Dasher's preferred language.

3

Resolve or route

Approved answers where safe; escalation for sensitive issues.

4

Write back

Recap, next step, and structured ops outcome.

Metrics to validate

Operator-owned
1

Activation recovery

Does the workflow bring stalled Dashers back?

2

Repeat contacts

Does it reduce unresolved support loops?

3

Escalation quality

Are sensitive issues routed with the right context?

4

Follow-up completion

Does ops get a useful structured outcome?

Example outbound

Ask for a pressure-test, not a transformation meeting.

The message is deliberately narrow: current AI signal, one workflow hypothesis, measurable operating outcomes, and an easy first conversation.

Hi {{FirstName}},

I saw DoorDash has been moving quickly on AI commerce, from Ask DoorDash to grocery/cart-building and AI-assisted merchant workflows.

One workflow I would like to pressure-test is Dasher activation after onboarding or an early delivery. My hypothesis is that some new Dashers stall because a payout, Fast Pay, background-check, or first-delivery trust issue never gets resolved clearly.

Worth comparing whether OpenAI could support a narrow voice workflow here, measured on activation recovery, repeat support contacts, escalation quality, and follow-up completion?
