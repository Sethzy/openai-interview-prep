## Interview Answer Tone

Default all interview-answer drafts to a spoken, human tone. They should sound
like Seth thinking clearly in an interview, not like a polished memo, LinkedIn
summary, or corporate pitch.

Preferred style:

- Conversational openings are allowed: "Yeah, the way I think about it is...",
  "The honest answer is...", "For me...", or "The simplest way to explain it
  is..."
- Keep the language plain, warm, and serious without becoming stiff.
- Use lived examples before abstract claims.
- Make sales the governing language: customers, trust, proof, workflow,
  account, buyer map, risk, handoff, urgency, and next step.
- Keep one clean through-line, then add detail that Seth can cut live.
- Let the answers sound slightly imperfect in a useful way. Spoken credibility
  is better than over-polished phrasing.

Avoid:

- Formal application prose, consultant-speak, grand mission rhetoric, or brand
  worship.
- "I am uniquely positioned" and similar resume-summary language.
- Cramming every metric into the first answer.
- Treating AI interest as identity wallpaper. Tie it to work Seth has actually
  done: Codex, Second Brain, Salescraft, GTM Workspace, source-backed research,
  CRM hygiene, seller handoff, and review-first systems.
- Over-scripting answers so tightly that they would sound memorized on Zoom.

## Interview Prep Operating Loop

When Seth provides interview-pipeline context, capture it into this vault and
turn it into prep assets.

Default workflow:

1. Identify the next interview stage, interviewer type, and evaluation rubric.
2. Map that stage to likely tests: product fluency, enterprise sales judgment,
   discovery, account strategy, technical-buyer communication, regulated-buyer
   maturity, AI-enabled GTM workflow design, coachability, and motivation.
3. Pull the relevant evidence from the career-ops OpenAI dossier and Seth proof
   sources.
4. Draft speakable answers in 15-second, 30-second, 2-minute, and STAR forms
   when useful.
5. Maintain truth boundaries and confidence labels.
6. Update the wiki hot cache or an appropriate prep note after significant new
   context.

For the current loop, explicitly test the recruiter-screen rubric:

- Founding-team ambiguity, initiative, and process-building.
- Full-cycle closing evidence and depth of deal ownership.
- Strategic-account work with ADs, meeting leadership, and multi-threading.
- Executive presence and natural, original communication.
- Account tiering, stakeholder targeting, and strategic outbound planning.
- Simple technical explanations that end in business value.
- Competitive/product-fit judgment for accounts already using another product.
- Genuine AI curiosity demonstrated through concrete use and proof-of-work.

The main Seth-specific objection is career legibility: make the connection from
Boxo enterprise selling to Airwallex quota-carrying AE execution to founder-led
full-cycle AI sales explicit. Never require the interviewer to infer it.

Priority interview themes:

- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Why OpenAI?"
- "Why Strategic BDR rather than AE?"
- "How would you qualify a product-signal-led enterprise opportunity?"
- "How would you map stakeholders in a regulated APAC account?"
- "How would you partner with an Account Director?"
- "How would you build GPTs/Agents for prospecting and qualification?"
- "How do ChatGPT Enterprise, API, agents, Codex, company knowledge, MCP/apps,
  security/admin controls, and spend governance connect to business value?"
- "How would you approach a Singapore bank, insurer, life-sciences company,
  retailer, super-app, or public-sector account?"
