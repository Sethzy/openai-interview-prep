# OpenAI SDR Evidence And Verification Ledger

Internal truth-control ledger for Seth's OpenAI Strategic BDR APAC interview prep.

Use this page before final rehearsal, Loom recording, screen share, or recruiter/final-round conversations. It separates:

- `Safe to say`: evidence-backed and interview-appropriate.
- `Say with caveat`: directionally useful but exact basis needs confirmation.
- `Verify before saying`: likely to change or account/product-specific.
- `Do not say`: sensitive, unverified, or not Seth's fact.

## Read This First

- This is internal. Do not screen-share this page.
- The screen-share-safe surface is [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]].
- Use Stanley/friend/process artifacts for prep intelligence only, not as official OpenAI facts.
- Verify current OpenAI product/security/model claims from official OpenAI sources before live use.
- Verify target-account facts from primary/current sources before any case submission.
- Do not name the warm path, claim insider access, or discuss source-reported comp/process details unless Seth explicitly chooses to.

## Status Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Safe to say | CV-backed, Seth-specific, or official/current enough for interview use. |
| Say with caveat | Useful, but exact number, wording, or scope needs Seth confirmation. |
| Verify before saying | Time-sensitive, product-sensitive, account-sensitive, or source-sensitive. |
| Do not say | Unverified, private, source-reported, Stanley-specific, or overclaims OpenAI. |

## Before Each Interview

Run this check:

1. Open [[OpenAI-SDR-comprehensive-answer-expansion-bank]] and the one stage-specific artifact.
2. Check whether the stage requires product, metric, account, or process facts.
3. For product/security/model claims, verify against official current OpenAI sources.
4. For account facts, verify against primary company sources.
5. For metrics, use only safe numbers or caveat.
6. For case study, verify actual prompt and deadline.
7. Close internal pages before screen share.
8. Use [[OpenAI-SDR-rapid-fire-mock-and-delivery-drill]] for the stage circuit.

## Do-Not-Say List

- Stanley's personal facts, employers, achievements, or title-step-back story as Seth facts.
- Friend/process intelligence as official OpenAI fact.
- Warm path identity or internal referral details.
- Compensation, hiring count, start dates, promotion timeline, or reference-check signal unless recruiter confirms.
- Any claim of insider access.
- Specific OpenAI model names or product claims not verified from current official sources.
- "100% no hallucinations."
- Security, privacy, compliance, residency, or healthcare guarantees not officially confirmed.
- Discount-led claims.
- "OpenAI is simply better than everyone."
- A named lost deal that Seth has not confirmed.
