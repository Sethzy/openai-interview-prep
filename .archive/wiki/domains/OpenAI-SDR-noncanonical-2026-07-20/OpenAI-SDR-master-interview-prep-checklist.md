## Read This First

This is the internal master checklist for Seth's OpenAI Strategic Business Development Representative - APAC interview prep.

Use it as a workbook:

- check off each item only when the answer is written, rehearsed, and backed by Seth-specific proof;
- keep source-reported process details private unless recruiter or interviewer confirms them;
- use Stanley artifacts for structure, not facts;
- verify current OpenAI product, security, privacy, compliance, model, and pricing claims before saying them live;
- keep compensation, hiring-count, start-date, promotion-path, reference-signal, and warm-path details out of any screen-share-safe prep doc.

Status labels:

- `Not started`: no answer yet.
- `Drafted`: written but not rehearsed.
- `Rehearsed`: speakable without reading.
- `Verified`: factual claims checked against primary/current sources.
- `Ready`: rehearsed, verified, concise, and tied to Seth proof.

Priority labels:

- `P0`: must nail before the next round.
- `P1`: high-likelihood or high-value.
- `P2`: useful coverage for edge cases.

Source-confidence labels:

- `Official-for-Stanley`: from Stanley's OpenAI prompt email, but not automatically Seth's exact prompt.
- `Friend-reported`: from the process briefing, high-value but not official.
- `Stanley-structure`: from Stanley's prep notes, useful as structure only.
- `Derived`: inferred from the JD, role dossier, or repeated interview themes.
- `Needs-current-verification`: likely to change or requires official/current sourcing.
