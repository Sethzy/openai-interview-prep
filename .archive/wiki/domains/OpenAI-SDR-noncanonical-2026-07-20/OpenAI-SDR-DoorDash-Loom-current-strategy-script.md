## 3.5-Minute Strategy Script

> My three-year AI point of view for DoorDash is that AI should turn local-commerce growth into operating leverage.
>
> DoorDash is already using AI in visible ways: discovery, recommendations, merchant onboarding, cart-building, and the ChatGPT grocery experience. I would not ignore that. But the larger enterprise opportunity is to make the marketplace itself cheaper and easier to run. DoorDash is not a pure software business. It is a three-sided operating system across consumers, merchants, and Dashers, and every market adds local language, support, trust, payout, merchant, and logistics complexity.
>
> So I would not start with a generic AI assistant. I would start with a narrow, measurable operating workflow: Dasher activation and reactivation. The hypothesis is simple. Some new Dashers stall after onboarding or after an early delivery because a payout, Fast Pay, background-check, scheduling, or first-delivery trust issue never gets resolved clearly. DoorDash has already spent effort acquiring and onboarding that Dasher, but has not converted that effort into reliable supply.
>
> The OpenAI use case is a multilingual local-ops voice layer. It can call a stalled Dasher, answer approved questions in the Dasher's preferred language, identify the reason they stopped, route sensitive issues like pay, safety, fraud, or account decisions to a human, send a recap, and write a structured outcome back to operations. The value is not "voice AI" by itself. The value is better activation recovery, fewer repeat support loops, cleaner escalations, and more consistent local execution.
>
> In the first 30 days, I would turn this from an account thesis into a validated POC path. Days 1 to 5, I would build the DoorDash thesis from public AI launches, operating pressure, and the activation workflow. Days 6 to 10, I would align with the OpenAI account team on proof points, security boundaries, and what not to claim. Days 11 to 20, I would validate the pain with a DoorDash operator in Dasher activation, Dasher experience, supply quality, support, or marketplace operations. By day 30, the output should be a narrow POC proposal with success metrics, guardrails, and a simulated workflow artifact operators can critique.
>
> I would target the operating pain owner first, not the most senior executive with a broad AI pitch. The first conversation should answer: where do new Dashers drop off, what questions create repeat contacts, what manual follow-up exists today, and what metric would make a pilot matter? Once that pain is real, I would multi-thread into AI/ML, platform, trust and safety, privacy/security, and finance so the conversation can move from useful demo to governed enterprise opportunity.
>
> I would earn credibility by respecting DoorDash's existing AI and internal ML work. OpenAI should be positioned as a way to extend that momentum into a specific workflow, not replace DoorDash's roadmap. The demo I would show is bounded: no real DoorDash data, no production system access, approved answers only, structured writeback, evals, and human escalation for high-risk issues.
>
> What I would do differently from other candidates is bring a researched account thesis and a working proof artifact. The demo is not the final product. It is a conversation artifact that lets an operator say, "yes, this is the workflow" or "no, the real pain is somewhere else." That is how I would turn the Loom from a pitch into the beginning of an Account Director-ready opportunity.

## Use Case Spine

### One-Sentence Thesis

> DoorDash should use OpenAI to build a multilingual local-operations voice layer that helps recover stalled Dashers, reduce repeat support loops, and turn local activation friction into structured operational insight.

### Why This Is DoorDash-Specific

- DoorDash is a three-sided marketplace, so consumer demand only matters if merchant quality and Dasher supply are reliable.
- DoorDash already has visible AI momentum in consumer discovery, cart-building, recommendations, and merchant onboarding.
- Dasher activation is a practical operating workflow where AI can be measured against recovery, support, escalation, and follow-up outcomes.
- The voice demo makes the workflow concrete enough for operators to critique.

### Business Priorities To Lead With

- Recover stalled Dashers before acquisition/onboarding effort is wasted.
- Reduce repeat support contacts and improve escalation quality.
- Repair trust around pay, Fast Pay, safety, first-delivery issues, and return-to-dash moments.
- Make local support more consistent across languages and markets.
- Capture structured outcomes for operations instead of leaving context in unstructured calls and manual notes.

## How To Earn Credibility

- Start with DoorDash's existing AI momentum.
- Respect that DoorDash likely has strong internal ML, product, and operations teams.
- Do not pitch "OpenAI can help with AI" broadly.
- Show a specific operating workflow and explain why it matters commercially.
- Bring frontline activation context from high-volume workforce workflows.
- Make the guardrails explicit: pay, safety, fraud, and account decisions escalate to humans.
- Treat the demo as a critique artifact, not production software.

## Do Not Say

- Do not say DoorDash is not already using AI.
- Do not say OpenAI would replace DoorDash search, recommendations, support, or internal ML.
- Do not claim the agent can autonomously resolve pay, safety, fraud, background-check, or account decisions.
- Do not claim real DoorDash data, real integration, real production-readiness, or measured ROI.
- Do not lead with market exits or thin margins in first-touch outreach.
- Do not use exact OpenAI model, residency, pricing, latency, or contract claims unless verified.

## Claims To Recheck Before Recording

- DoorDash public AI initiatives used in the opening.
- DoorDash Q1 2026 scale and earnings/order figures.
- DoorDash FY2025 profit/order economics if mentioned.
- DoorDash current country footprint after reported exits from Qatar, Singapore, Japan, and Uzbekistan.
- Exact OpenAI voice/audio product names, security boundaries, eval/guardrail language, and enterprise claims.
- Whether the actual prompt requires DoorDash, allows DoorDash, or assigns a different account.
