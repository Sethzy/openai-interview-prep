OpenAI Interview Process Overview
Multi-round process: HR screen, case study (Loom), 3rd round (SDR manager), 4th round series (AE x2), 5th round (2 VPs)
Turnaround between rounds: 1 to 3 business days; faster response = good sign
Reference check request = near-certain pass signal
Full process can complete in roughly 2 to 3 weeks if moving quickly
Onboarding cohorts are fixed: 20th July or 3rd August start dates
Hiring 6 SDRs total; targeting close by end of July; 2 offers confirmed so far

Case Study: Loom Video (Round 2)
5 to 7 minute Loom recording presenting a slide deck (PowerPoint, Canva, anything works)
Case: how should a company (e.g. Uber) use AI, plus one OpenAI use case pitch
Slides are not mandatory but recommended as the clearest format
Reviewed by VP of Sales, not the SDR manager
Prep time: allow a full day just for recording; Loom does allow editing but awkward gaps will show

Round 3: SDR Manager (JT)
Pitch OpenAI or agents generically; know your stuff but no need to be super technical
Walk through a deal end to end
Describe your prospecting motion
Interviewer will review your Loom recording and ask follow-up questions on it
Willingness to learn and humility are tested explicitly
Regional nuance question: what is different about prospecting in SEA vs. globally?
India: cost sensitive
ANZ and Singapore: tech-forward, more mature
Channel differences: email vs. cold call vs. local apps (Zalo in Vietnam, etc.)
“If you could pick one APAC market to land in first, which would it be and why?”

Round 4: AE Interview Series (Two Back-to-Back, 30 Min Each)
Scheduled same day, back to back; interviewers are enterprise AEs (Aussie and US-based, ~2 years at OpenAI)
Technical Fluency round
Can hallucination be 100% avoided?
What is RAG? (RAG comes up a lot; treat it as a big topic)
How would you pitch a prospect already using Copilot?
How do we compare and win against Anthropic?
Genuine curiosity question: why take a step back to SDR?
Commercial Acumen round
Elements of consultative and value-based selling: lead with priorities, pain points, challenges; never lead with “our product is superior” or cheaper pricing
Walk me through a deal end to end: high level only, focus on sales approach (value-based etc.), multi-threading, how you navigated challenges, what you learned
Metrics they will ask: average deal size, run-rate deals per month, deals closed per month, your target
Scenario question: if you joined OpenAI tomorrow, which companies would you target first and why?
What value driver would you land with?
Competing against Anthropic angle: “Great that you’re already using Anthropic. I’m not here to rip and replace. Let’s explore where OpenAI can land within your current stack and expand from there.” Lean on OpenAI’s wider product suite (Whisper, Sora, etc.)

Round 5: VP Series (Two Separate Sessions)

RAG and hallucination questions (same as round 4, repeated)
Sales situational questions: what campaign would you run first, messaging approach, multi-threading
Digs into deal specifics: average deal size, run-rate pipeline, deals closed per month, quota
Use the STAR template for situational answers
Be upfront about SME/high-velocity background vs. enterprise; she appreciates honesty

Walk through each experience: what you did, what you learned, why you left
What playbook did you build? What worked?
Founding team experience (e.g. Figma) will be probed: what systems did you set up from scratch?
No technical product questions; purely sales methodology and career narrative
Both VPs: they have a clear picture of the SDR profile they want
AE-calibre person taking a deliberate step back for OpenAI specifically
Consultative and value-based seller
Humble and willing to learn
Do not be a challenger or try to be “outside the box”; fit the mould
