# OpenAI Loom Project Prompt Photo

This image is a photo of the OpenAI Loom case assignment prompt. It visually duplicates the core assignment text extracted from the PDF email thread.

## Visible Prompt Structure

Part 1, 1-2 minutes: "Why You? Why OpenAI?"

The candidate should introduce themself as if speaking to the hiring panel and briefly cover:

- Why OpenAI.
- Why this role.
- What makes the candidate a strong fit.
- Why the candidate would thrive in the environment.
- OpenAI is looking for conviction, self-awareness, humility, ambition, and authenticity.

Part 2, 5 minutes: "Advise a Strategic Customer"

The candidate should imagine they have joined OpenAI's Sales Development team and need to develop an account strategy for DoorDash that creates executive interest in partnering with OpenAI. The prompt asks for:

1. A point of view on how DoorDash should use AI to strengthen its business or unlock future growth over the next 3 years.
2. One high-impact OpenAI use case for DoorDash, with examples including customer experience, merchant growth, Dasher operations, internal productivity, support, and logistics.
3. A 30-day executive engagement plan covering who to target first, what business priorities to lead with, how to earn credibility, what outreach would sound like, and at least one outbound messaging example.
4. A standout approach: what the candidate would do that others likely would not, and what strengths would give them an edge.

Final note:

OpenAI says it is building for the long term and looking for future sellers, advisors, and leaders.
