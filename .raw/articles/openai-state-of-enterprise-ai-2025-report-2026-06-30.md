# Raw Snapshot: The State Of Enterprise AI 2025 Report

Official source: https://openai.com/index/the-state-of-enterprise-ai-2025-report/

## Page Identity

OpenAI published "The State of Enterprise AI 2025" as a report on business AI
adoption, co-developed with Wharton. The page frames the report around lessons
from organizations moving AI from experimentation toward business use.

## Extracted Claims

- The report is based on a large global AI-focused survey of more than 9,000
  leaders and employees across major industries and markets, plus expert and
  executive interviews.
- OpenAI reports that AI use increased substantially since 2024.
- OpenAI reports that a large majority of employees say AI improves efficiency.
- OpenAI reports that leaders estimate a meaningful share of employees use AI
  daily.
- OpenAI reports that most organizations seeing adoption report ROI and
  workforce improvements.
- The report's enterprise-adoption frame is that the advantage comes from
  integrating AI into real workflows, not merely providing isolated access.
- The report emphasizes that more mature AI users treat AI as a thought
  partner and operating tool, not only as a content generator.
- The report is useful for the internal-build objection because it supports the
  idea that adoption maturity matters: buyers need workflows, governance,
  training, measurement, and operating cadence, not only internal model
  experimentation.

## Interview-Relevant Interpretation

The report strengthens the answer that OpenAI is selling help with enterprise
adoption depth. The question is not only whether a company can build AI
internally. The question is whether AI is being integrated into workflows in a
way that improves business outcomes, governance, and employee behavior.

## Guardrail

Do not cite report percentages live unless rechecked immediately before the
interview. Use the report mainly for the adoption-maturity thesis: enterprise
AI value comes from workflow integration, measurement, and behavior change.
