# Raw Snapshot: The Next Phase Of Enterprise AI

Official source: https://openai.com/index/next-phase-of-enterprise-ai/

## Page Identity

OpenAI frames the next phase of enterprise AI around making ChatGPT understand
business context, perform work across connected systems, and improve through
evaluation and optimization.

## Extracted Claims

- OpenAI says enterprise AI is moving from simple assistance toward systems
  that can understand business context, take action, and be evaluated.
- Business Context is framed as bringing relevant company context from
  connected systems into ChatGPT while respecting permissions in those systems.
- Agent Execution is framed as allowing ChatGPT to perform pre-authorized
  workflows through controlled connectors and MCP-style integrations.
- Evaluation and Optimization is framed as making AI behavior measurable and
  improvable before scale.
- The article makes the enterprise story more concrete than "chatbot" or
  "model access": context, action, and measurement become the work surface.
- The article is useful for the internal-build objection because it names the
  layers internal teams often have to build or govern themselves: business
  context, system access, workflow execution, evaluation, and optimization.

## Interview-Relevant Interpretation

This is the clearest source for explaining why OpenAI partnership can matter
even when a buyer has an internal AI team. The partnership is not just model
quality. It is helping teams move from generic chat and prototypes into
business-context-aware, tool-using, measurable workflows.
