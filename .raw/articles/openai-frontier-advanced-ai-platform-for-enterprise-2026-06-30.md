# Raw Snapshot: OpenAI Frontier

Official source: https://openai.com/business/frontier/

## Page Identity

OpenAI presents Frontier as an advanced AI platform for enterprise customers.
The page frames Frontier as access to the same broad product power behind
ChatGPT, Codex, and Sora, combined with technical expertise and flexible
deployment options.

## Extracted Claims

- Frontier brings together OpenAI models and products, one-to-one technical
  expertise, and flexible deployment options for companies ready to unlock AI
  at enterprise scale.
- The page highlights access to OpenAI's latest AI innovations, including
  product releases and research previews.
- The page frames direct collaboration with OpenAI experts as part of the
  enterprise value proposition. It references customer success, solutions
  engineering, deployment engineering, and research expertise.
- The page separates two enterprise paths:
  - Advanced AI platform: access to OpenAI models and products such as ChatGPT,
    Codex, Sora, API, and tools.
  - Custom deployments: expert-supported design, build, and integration of AI
    agents and applications.
- Custom deployment language points toward agents and applications built around
  the customer's context, systems, controls, and business processes.
- The page is useful for the internal-build objection because it frames OpenAI
  as more than a model API. OpenAI is presenting enterprise value as platform
  access plus deployment expertise.

## Interview-Relevant Interpretation

For a buyer with internal AI teams, Frontier supports a hybrid answer: the
customer can own differentiated workflow logic and proprietary context, while
OpenAI supplies platform primitives, frontier product access, implementation
patterns, and technical support.

## Guardrail

Do not promise that every Strategic BDR account receives Frontier, custom
deployments, FDE support, research previews, or specific deployment resources.
Use the page as directional evidence for OpenAI's enterprise platform and
deployment-support strategy.
