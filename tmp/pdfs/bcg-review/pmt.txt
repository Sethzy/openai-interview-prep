What does Knowledge AI help a client do that they can’t do with OpenAI? What
are its benefits?

Our Knowledge AI toolkit is built to be vendor agnostic, so we leverage OpenAI and other Large Language Models (LLMs)
vendors as needed including competitors like Cohere, Google, and Hugging Face…

In any case, BCG/X believes that:




                                                                                                                                       Copyright © 2022 by Boston Consulting Group. All rights reserved.
 • 10% of a solution is the LLM/Models
 • 20% is the IT integration and customization
 • 70% is change management

We don’t have to use Knowledge AI however; we believe Knowledge AI it has many benefits that we can leverage for our
clients including:
•   Ease of integration: the toolkit simplifies the process of incorporating LLMs into a final solution, allowing developers to
    quickly leverage LLMs capabilities without having to spend time on the low-level details of LLM integration (e.g.: data
    sources, additional LLMs beyond OpenAI).
•   Improved productivity: by providing a higher-level of abstraction, the toolkit helps developers build applications more
    efficiently, which in turn can result in faster time-to-market for new products and features.
•   Customization: the toolkit offers customization options and configurations tailored to the contact center where we
    initially deployed it, making it easier to adapt the LLM technology to their specific needs.
•   LLM scalability: the toolkit addresses some scalability concerns, such as efficiently managing and optimizing LLM token
    usage, it can help us build applications that can handle growth in user demand more effectively.

Knowledge AI leverages Augmented Language Models (ALMs) to provide state of
the art reasoning and solutions to a large type of problem.


     ALMs are LLMs with enhanced reasoning skills that can use tools to
     elaborate their answers.




                                                                                   Copyright © 2022 by Boston Consulting Group. All rights reserved.
     Tools such as search engines, calculators, code interpreters, database
     queries, APIs, etc. can help LLMs produce factual answers.

     ALMs learn to reason, use tools and outperform regular LLMs on
     benchmarks.
